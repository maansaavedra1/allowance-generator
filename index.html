V1 · Bracket-Based
Bracket-Based Overtime Allowance Generator
Computes OT allowances based on rendered overtime hours following your company's policy. Set up allowance types with configurable hour-bracket tiers, define who qualifies through employee field filters, and generate payroll-ready outputs in one click.

V2 · Per-Hour Rate
Special Work Assignment Allowance Generator
Computes allowances for special work assignments based on approved OT hours and a per-hour rate. Weekday OT applies a fixed rate, while weekend OT derives the rate from the employee's base salary with a configurable premium — ensuring accurate, policy-aligned compensation.

V3 · Tier-Based
Daily Allowance Generator
Computes daily Food, Transportation, and Hazard Pay allowances by employee tier, based on which attendance status (Onsite, WFH, Late, Leave, etc.) qualifies for each allowance type. Attendance-source wiring is manual for now — day counts are entered per employee.
